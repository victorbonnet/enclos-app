# Needs you combines requests without changing their authority

Questions and held tasks are easy to miss inside conversations, while policy requests already have a dedicated Inbox section. Needs you replaces Sandbox requests with one unfolded list before conversations whenever requests wait, preserving the original controls and each host's authority instead of introducing another decision queue. A complete per-host projection and cached discovery references keep reconnects and other devices consistent without treating an unavailable source as an empty Inbox.

Expired ordinary questions remain discoverable but read-only, with View conversation and explicit host-persisted Dismiss reminder. They offer no answer controls or late-reply draft. This keeps expiry unambiguous and avoids a late answer interfering with a newer Hermes question. Runtime approvals retain their exact native lifetime and also stay read-only after expiry. Suggested replies are ordinary literal messages, never approval controls.

A focused request sheet keeps reviewing a reminder separate from conversation history. Dismiss reminder applies to every source kind as a per-person, source-version-bound discovery preference; it does not mutate the canonical request. Viewing a held task uses its creator and exact board reference, not a guessed mapping to the assignee's task list.
